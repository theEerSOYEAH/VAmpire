# Vampirity

A 2D Roguelike game made with godot

## About

You spawn in a wierd monochrome looking forest with a pistol and a gun. You need to fight off hoards of all types of enemies that try to attack you while trying to dodge their attacks. Find new weapons that drop from elite enemies and collect potions to get stronger. You have the ability to dash and while dashing you become immune to all attacks.

## How To Run The Game

Head over to this itch.io link -> https://mubixd.itch.io/vampirity

## How To Play

* WASD - To Move
* Left Click - To Attack
* Space - To Dash
* Q - To Change Weapons

## Potions
* Health Potions -- Recovers some of your health
* Speed Potions -- Makes player move faster
* Luck Potions -- Boosts chance of potions spawning

## Elite Enemies

These enemies are bigger and stronger than usual. Although if killed you will get rewarded.
