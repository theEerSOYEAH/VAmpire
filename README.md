A 2D Roguelike game made with godot

## About

You spawn in a wierd monochrome looking forest with a pistol and a sword. You need to fight off hoards of all types of enemies that try to attack you while trying to dodge their attacks. Kill enemies to collect XP and level up, then pick an upgrade card to get stronger. Find new weapons that drop from elite enemies, grab ammo and potions, and survive as long as you can. You have the ability to dash and while dashing you become immune to all attacks.

## How To Play

* WASD - To Move
* Left Click - To Attack
* Space - To Dash (has a cooldown, shown on screen)
* Q - To Change Weapons
* R - To Reload

## Weapons

* Pistol -- Your starting gun. Decent fire rate, ammo is easy to find
* Sword -- Melee weapon, no ammo needed
* AK-47 -- Fully automatic and hits harder than the pistol. Dropped by the first elite enemy
* Shotgun -- Fires a spread of pellets, great up close. Dropped by the second elite enemy
* Knife -- Unlocked from a level up card. It orbits around you and automatically throws itself at nearby enemies

Each gun has its own magazine and ammo count.

## Leveling Up

Killing enemies drops XP gems. Collect enough XP and you level up, the game pauses and you pick one of three upgrade cards:

* Health Card -- +1 max health and heals you for 1
* Speed Card -- Permanently makes you move faster
* Knife Card -- Unlocks the orbiting knife

Every level needs more XP than the last, so choose wisely.

## Potions
* Health Potions -- Recovers some of your health
* Speed Potions -- Makes player move faster for a short time (a timer and bar show how long is left)
* Luck Potions -- Boosts chance of drops spawning

## Pickups

* Ammo Boxes -- Refill ammo for the pistol, AK-47 and shotgun
* Nuke -- Instantly wipes out every enemy on screen
* Freeze -- Freezes all enemies in place for a few seconds
* Magnet -- Pulls in nearby drops and XP gems

## Enemies

### Normal Enemies -- Chase you down and hurt you on contact
* Normality -> Just a normal enemy
* Quickity -> A fast but low-health enemy
* Tankity -> A slow but high-health enemy
* Webity -> A mix of Quickity and Normality
* Undeathity -> A mix of Normality and Tankity
* Noobity -> Summoned by Summinity
### Ranged Enemies -- Keep their distance and charge up projectiles to shoot at you. They sometimes drop luck potions
* Cruelty
### Summoners -- Get close to them and they will spawn a group of smaller enemies around them
* Summinity 

## Elite Enemies

These enemies are bigger and stronger than usual. Although if killed you will get rewarded.
